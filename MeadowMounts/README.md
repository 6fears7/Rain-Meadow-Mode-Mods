Carry and ride other creatures in Rain Meadow's Meadow mode

Use Grab to grab, and Special to drop.

Lizards can carry one creature on their back, besides Red Lizards and Trains, which can both carry 3.

All lizards can carry an additional creature in the mouth. Just bite them with Grab.
